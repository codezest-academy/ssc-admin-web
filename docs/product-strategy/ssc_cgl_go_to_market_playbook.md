# SSC CGL: Go-To-Market & Growth Playbook

This document details the exact execution strategies for Phases 2 & 3 of our Product Growth Framework, specifically focusing on Content Creation, SEO, Social Media, and Analytics.

---

## 1. Content Strategy (Step 8)
**Goal:** Build a moat of high-value, high-intent educational content that cannot easily be replicated.

*   **Distractor Rationales:** Every mock test question must have an explanation not just for the correct answer, but for the common traps (e.g., "If you got 12.5, you forgot to multiply the base by 2"). This is our core product differentiator.
*   **Micro-Topic Deep Dives:** Move away from generic "Maths Notes." Create hyper-focused PDF/Video content on micro-topics like *'Successive Percentage Changes'* or *'Important SSC CGL Error Detection Rules'* that aspirants actively struggle with.
*   **Previous Year Question (PYQ) Mastery:** Tag every PYQ with its exact shift and date. Aspirants revere PYQs; organizing them better than the competition is a massive growth lever.

## 2. Programmatic SEO & Organic Growth (Step 9)
**Goal:** Dominate Google search results for long-tail, high-intent SSC CGL queries.

*   **Programmatic PYQ Pages:** We will dynamically generate thousands of SEO-optimized pages for every single PYQ in our database. 
    *   *URL Structure:* `codezest.academy/ssc-cgl/pyq/2023/tier-1/shift-2/maths/question-15`
    *   *Content:* The question, the solution, the distractor rationale, and a CTA (Call to Action) to "Practice similar questions for free."
*   **Syllabus & Notification Pillars:** Rank #1 for terms like "SSC CGL Syllabus 2026", "SSC CGL Salary", and "SSC CGL Notification". These pages must be heavily interlinked to our free diagnostic tests.
*   **Diagnostic SEO:** Target queries like "How to improve maths speed for SSC CGL." The landing page offers the free 10-minute diagnostic test as the lead magnet.

## 3. Social Media & Community Hijacking (Step 10)
**Goal:** Infiltrate existing communities where aspirants spend their time (Telegram & YouTube).

*   **The Telegram "Diagnostic" Bot:** Build or manage a Telegram channel that posts a daily "1-Question Challenge". When a user answers, the bot links them to the platform for a full 10-minute revision set.
*   **YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels:** 
    *   *Format:* 60-second videos showing a "Topper's Trick" vs "Average Student Approach".
    *   *Hook:* "Are you stuck at 130 marks?"
    *   *CTA:* "Take the free AI diagnostic test (link in bio) to find out exactly why."
*   **Influencer Partnerships:** Sponsor micro-influencers (educators with 10k-50k subscribers) to take our mock test live and showcase the advanced analytics dashboard.

## 4. Measurement & Analytics (Step 11)
**Goal:** Track the full funnel to ensure our USP is actually driving conversions.

*   **Acquisition Metrics:**
    *   Cost Per Acquisition (CAC) vs. Organic Traffic Volume.
    *   Sign-up conversion rate on Programmatic SEO pages.
*   **Activation Metrics (The "Aha" Moment):**
    *   Percentage of new sign-ups who complete their first free diagnostic test. (Target: > 60%).
*   **Retention Metrics (PLG Loops):**
    *   DAU/MAU Ratio.
    *   Completion rate of "Daily 10-Min Revision" streaks.
*   **Monetization Metrics:**
    *   Free-to-Paid conversion rate (how many users buy the premium mock test package after seeing their initial analytics report).

## 5. Continuous Improvement Loop (Step 12)
**Goal:** Use data to iteratively refine the MVP.

*   **Drop-off Analysis:** If 40% of users abandon the mock test halfway, we must introduce a "Pause & Save" feature or shorten the default test length.
*   **Content Iteration:** If the analytics show users constantly failing "Trigonometry Identities," we aggressively push out a free mini-course on that specific topic to drive goodwill and retention.
*   **Feature Expansion:** Once the diagnostic MVP is stable, expand into live Leaderboards based on *Improvement Percentage* rather than raw scores, fostering a highly motivated, engaged community.
